How to flash

  1. 1 Connect the board to your computer with a USB data cable.
  2. 2 Click Install for your hardware and select the board's serial port.
  3. 3 Wait for the installer to finish. The board restarts with the new firmware.

Board not showing up, or first-time setup? See the flashing guide.

Flashing runs in your browser with ESP Web Tools.
