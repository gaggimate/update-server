How installing works

  1. 1 Connect the board to this computer with a USB cable that can carry data, not just power.
  2. 2 Press Install for your hardware and pick the board's serial port when the browser asks.
  3. 3 Keep the cable connected until the installer says it is done. The board then restarts on the new firmware.

The board doesn't show up, or you are setting it up for the first time? Read the flashing guide.

Firmware is written over USB by your browser using ESP Web Tools. Nothing from your computer is uploaded.
